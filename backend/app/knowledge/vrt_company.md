- Founders, CEOs, owners and executive teams of SMBs, typically US-based, $2M-$10M revenue,

## What we offer
- **VEGA(c) (Visionary Entrepreneurs Growth Accelerator)** -- cohort-based accelerator.
  In-person workshops in several US cities (Bay Area, Austin, Connecticut, Miami, Cleveland,
  Atlanta); the site describes both 3-day and 4-day formats, so do NOT quote a day count.
  Covers 10 modules (Change or Die, Trust Advantage, Dynamic Communication, Stages of
  Business Growth, Strategic Planning and Execution, Growth As A Process, Vision/Purpose/
  Values/Norms, Emotional Intelligence, Hiring/Talent/Succession Planning, Power of
  Reflection) plus ongoing mentoring. Steps: Diagnose, Build, Execute. Two leaders per
  organization is recommended. Pricing is not public -- never quote one.
- **EGOS(c) (Entrepreneur Growth Operating System)** -- a customized operating system for one
  company: accountability structures so the business executes without founder dependency.
- **7 Stages of Growth Assessment** -- finds which growth stage (1-10 up to 161-500
  employees) the business is in, reveals hidden friction, shows the Builder vs. Protector
  mindset ratio, and gives a stage-specific action plan. Complimentary.
- **TriMetrix HD** -- personal performance assessment: behavior under pressure, motivators,
  leadership competencies, blind spots. Complimentary with the offer package.
- **Job Benchmarking** -- role-fit and talent-alignment diagnostic (with Master Job Profiles).
- **Business Academy** -- implementation-focused training in a 4-level leadership continuum
  (emerging managers, functional leaders, executive teams, founders/owners).
- **Free 45-minute Growth Strategy Call** with a VEGA advisor (also includes the two
  assessments for businesses with 20-500 employees). This is the call to invite people to.
