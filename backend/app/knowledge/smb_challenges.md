# The 27 core challenges SMBs face

Source: provided by VRT Management Group. This table is the single source of
truth: `loader.py` parses it, and the personalized-email prompt and the
company-research step both use these ids. Keep the table format (`| id | name | what it means |`)
when editing -- add or reword rows freely, but a test checks all 27 still parse.

| # | Business challenge | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hiring Quality Staff | Difficulty finding and hiring people who are the right fit for the role and business. |
| 2 | Staff Buy-In | Employees are not fully aligned with or committed to important strategies and initiatives. |
| 3 | No Systems in Place | Processes and systems do not keep up as the company grows. |
| 4 | Problem Diagnosis | Leadership struggles to identify the actual root cause behind business problems. |
| 5 | Business / Profit Design | The business model or profit structure is not producing sustainable profitability. |
| 6 | Employee Turnover | Valuable employees leave, creating disruption, replacement costs, and knowledge loss. |
| 7 | Project Management | Projects, resources, responsibilities, and execution are poorly coordinated. |
| 8 | Chaotic Periods | Rapid growth or operational pressure creates periods of confusion and instability. |
| 9 | How the Company Will Grow | Leadership and employees lack clarity about how the business should grow in the future. |
| 10 | Staff Satisfaction | The company does not fully understand or manage the connection between employee satisfaction and business performance. |
| 11 | Communication Gap | Leadership, managers, and employees do not communicate effectively. |
| 12 | Flexible Planning | Plans are too rigid to respond effectively when market or business conditions change. |
| 13 | Cost of Lost Expertise | The company loses valuable knowledge and experience when employees leave. |
| 14 | Market Changes Quickly | Customer expectations and market conditions change faster than the company can respond. |
| 15 | Staff Morale | Employees lack energy, motivation, engagement, or enthusiasm. |
| 16 | Inadequate Profits | The business is generating insufficient profit to support continued growth. |
| 17 | New Staff Orientation | New employees are not onboarded effectively enough to become productive and aligned. |
| 18 | Expanding Sales | The company lacks confidence, capability, or a repeatable approach for increasing sales. |
| 19 | Customer Migration | Existing customers move away from the company's products or services. |
| 20 | Staff Training | Employees do not receive sufficient training to perform and develop effectively. |
| 21 | Slow Product / Service Launch | The company takes too long to bring new products or services to market. |
| 22 | Cash Flow | The business experiences ongoing difficulty managing cash coming in and going out. |
| 23 | Difficulty Forecasting Problems | Leadership struggles to identify problems before they become serious. |
| 24 | Resistance to Change | Employees or leaders resist changes necessary for the business to evolve. |
| 25 | Unclear Values | Employees do not have a clear understanding of the company's values and how they should guide behavior. |
| 26 | Weak Market Differentiation | The company struggles to clearly differentiate its products or services from competitors. |
| 27 | Limited Capital | The company has insufficient financial resources to fund desired growth. |
